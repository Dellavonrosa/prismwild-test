PRISMWILD v0.15.52 • STATUS DAMAGE REWORK

1. Poison: 7% -> 10% of afflicted monster's max HP, dealt at the existing end-of-round tick.
2. Bleed: 6% max HP end-of-round tick REMOVED. Now recoils for 10% of damage actually dealt whenever the bleeding monster successfully attacks. Rounded to nearest HP, minimum 1 on a successful hit; overkill is capped to actual HP removed. No damage on a miss, dodge, or forfeited action.
3. Shock: now deals flat 10 HP at the existing end-of-round tick. Existing 25% Speed debuff and 15% chance of lost action preserved.
4. All attack-specific status chances and status durations remain exactly as authored. No automatic X-turn replacements.
5. Burn and other control statuses left alone.
6. Battle feedback shows Bleed recoil after attacks. Life Steal and Dodge feedback still show even when Bleed happens on the same move.
7. Silverwind Reference Guide explains the new mechanics.

Changed file: index.html only.
Unchanged: styles.css, content/, assets/, saved roster, Admin data.
