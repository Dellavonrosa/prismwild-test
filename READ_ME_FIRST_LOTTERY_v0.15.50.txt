PRISMWILD v0.15.50 LOTTERY • CORE MERGE PATCH

This is a MERGE PATCH, not a complete standalone build.
It contains only updated index.html, styles.css and these notes.

To install:
1. BACK UP your current prismwild-test repository or work folder.
2. Extract THIS ZIP INTO THE ROOT of the matching Prismwild v0.15.49 project/repository.
3. OVERWRITE only index.html and styles.css. Do not delete the existing content/ or assets/ folders.
4. For GitHub Pages, Commit and Push your changes and wait for deployment.
5. In-game, open Shop > Lottery Wheel. In Admin > Lottery Eggs, add species to the pool.
6. To publish your configured Lottery Egg monster pool to hosted players, use Admin > Export Live Package, extract that generated ZIP into your repo root, Commit and Push.

IMPORTANT: The LIVE_CONTENT module provided by Dell is used as the schema/reference for this patch. Its content/prismwild-content.js and existing assets are intentionally NOT replaced. Replacing it unnecessarily could overwrite newly published monsters or artwork.

Lottery Egg tokens from a win will wait safely in Inventory until their pool is configured. They can then be opened from Shop > Lottery or from Inventory.
