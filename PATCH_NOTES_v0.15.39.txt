PRISMWILD v0.15.39 — DYNAMIC HALLOWEEN MAZE ENCOUNTERS

HALLOWEEN MAZE
- Removed the fixed encounter-pool dependency that only allowed Dirgewake, Vesperfang, and Harrowjack.
- The Haunted Maze now scans the current species registry for published, non-Crossbreed monsters whose Obtain Method is "Halloween Maze Encounter".
- Grievwing is therefore eligible immediately from Dell's current authored content data.
- Existing eventSpeciesWeights remain valid for hand-tuned monsters.
- Newly tagged Maze species use a default weight of 40 unless a specific override is added later.
- The on-screen registered Event-monster count now uses the dynamic pool.
- The legacy eventSpecies list remains as a fallback only when no tagged species exist, preserving older saves/build assumptions.

CONTENT SAFETY
- content/prismwild-content.js is unchanged from v0.15.38.
- Roswyn / Trial Captain work from v0.15.38 is preserved.
