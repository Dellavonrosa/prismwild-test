Prismwild Prototype v0.15.47
Date: 2026-10-07

WEEKDAY MUSIC PLAYER
- Added a compact Music launcher directly under the Keeper name in the top bar.
- Added one zone theme for every weekday:
  Monday — Blaze Lands
  Tuesday — Flat Lands
  Wednesday — Shadow Plains
  Thursday — Bright Temple
  Friday — Permafrost Peaks
  Saturday — Wastelands
  Sunday — Chaos Wastes
- Music playback is user-initiated so browser autoplay restrictions are respected.
- Added persistent volume control. Default: 70%.
- Added Loop mode for continuous repeat.
- Added Ambient Fade mode: 2.5s fade-in, soft fade-out over the final ~6s, then a configurable quiet period before the track returns.
- Ambient quiet-period choices: 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45, or 60 minutes. Default: 10 minutes.
- Music preferences are stored separately in localStorage under prismwildMusic_v1 and do not alter player/game save data.
- The player checks for weekday rollover and switches to the appropriate day's theme.

No gameplay balance, monster data, breeding logic, or Live Content publishing behavior was changed.

Integrated content refresh:
- Bundled the latest Live Content revision from 2026-10-07.
- Rugaldrin now correctly uses Crag Rend per the supplied Live Content package.
