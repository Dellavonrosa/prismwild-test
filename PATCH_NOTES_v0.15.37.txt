PRISMWILD v0.15.37
Battle Attack Visual FX foundation

WHAT CHANGED
- Added reusable battle FX families: Bite, Slash, Slam, Projectile, Burst, Beam, and Wave.
- Attack Workshop now has a Visual FX selector with Auto plus explicit family overrides.
- Existing built-in + current custom attacks receive hand-tuned automatic family mappings, so old content works immediately without edits.
- Light / Standard / Heavy attack style now changes FX scale and impact weight.
- Every Element has a dedicated FX palette and particle treatment.
- Heavy successful hits add a brief battlefield kick; misses visually veer/whiff instead of showing a clean hit.
- Attack Registry now shows each attack's resolved FX family.
- The FX field is stored on custom attacks and therefore travels through normal Live Content export/import.

TEST CHECKLIST
1. Enter a battle and try attacks from several Elements and weight classes.
2. Confirm successful hits show an effect at the target and misses veer/whiff.
3. Confirm Heavy attacks feel larger and add a brief battlefield shake.
4. Open Admin -> Attack Editor and confirm Visual FX appears.
5. Load existing attacks and confirm their resolved FX family appears in the registry/preview.
6. Create or edit a custom attack, choose an explicit Visual FX family, save, then use it in battle.
7. Export Live Content after saving a custom attack and confirm normal publishing still works.

CONTENT SAFETY
The supplied current prismwild-content.js was preserved byte-for-byte in this build.
