Daily Expeditions

Send one to five available monsters on timed jobs across Prismwild. Longer expeditions bring home larger hauls, better rare-reward chances, and XP for each participating monster.

Three contracts each dayShort • 1 hourExtended • 5 hoursLong • 12 hours
