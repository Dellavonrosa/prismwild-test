Attack inheritance

The Mother-role monster contributes Attack Slot 1 and its Potential. The Father-role monster contributes Attack Slot 1 and its personality Trait. Every bred egg has a 5% chance to improve the inherited Potential by +1 stage, up to P12. Crossbreeds always hatch with their species Signature Attack in Slot 1.

Prismatic odds: 0.50% normally • 0.75% with one Pristine parent • 1.00% with two Pristine parents. Prismatic parents do not add a bonus.
